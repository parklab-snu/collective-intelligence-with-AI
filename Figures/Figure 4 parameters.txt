Climate and urbanisation showed contrasting associations with plumage polymorphism across among-city and within-city scales. Among cities, morph composition was primarily associated with climate, with colder cities showing higher melanisation, consistent with Bogert’s rule, , although the strength of this association varied across regions. Within cities, by contrast, urbanisation showed clearer associations than climate, with more urbanised grid cells tending to show higher melanisation and a more even morph composition. Humidity showed positive association with melanisation in both scales, however its association is small and weakly supported.
